Siebel Center Addition

(picture from 2023 feasibility study)

The Thomas M. Siebel Center for Computer Science Addition is an exciting new building for the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science. It will provide the school room to continue growing its faculty and staff, support additional graduate students, and create space for research in AI, data science, and a number of other focus areas. The University of Illinois Board of Trustees approved it in November 2025 with a preliminary budget of $78 million, paid for by university funds, gifts and state capital money through the Illinois Capital Development Board. As the project manager representing the school, I work with the Grainger College of Engineering, the university and the State of Illinois to ensure we deliver a state of the art facility up to the task of pursuing the Siebel School's research and instructional mission.

The Observatory

The Observatory is a web app that shows what you can actually see in the night sky from where you're standing, based on local light pollution and the time of night. My team built it as our final project for CS 411 (Database Systems), with a React frontend, a Flask API and a MySQL database in the cloud. I was the lead student scientist, wroting the advanced SQL queries at the core of the app. Those queries take a coordinate on Earth along with a timestamp, then work out which stars are above the horizon from there. That's what turns a big star catalog into a short list of things you can go outside and find.

InstructKG

InstructKG is an AI assistive tool for education. It reads course materials like lecture slides and PDFs, then builds a map of the key concepts and how they depend on each other, so students can see what to learn first and how topics connect. It was my first research project, done as a directed study at UIUC. I focused on the front end of the pipeline, improving how it takes in course materials (including scanned documents that used to come through with little or no readable text) and how well it pulls concepts out of them. I also ran and evaluated the pipeline on real UIUC course content using university computing resources.
